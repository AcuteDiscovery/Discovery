What Does Our 3-Star Rating Mean for You?

Texas Rising Star (TRS) is a voluntary quality rating and improvement system for child care programs in Texas. Achieving a 3-Star Certification means Discovery School voluntarily meets higher quality standards than standard state licensing requirements!

A Day at Discovery

A Typical Day of Fun & Learning

Routine helps young children feel secure, confident, and eager to participate.

6:30 AM

Warm Welcome & Free Play

Early arrivals enjoy tabletop toys, soft music, and quiet greetings as friends arrive.

8:00 AM

Nutritious Breakfast & Morning Circle

Morning meal followed by calendar time, weather, songs, and daily topic introductions.

9:30 AM

Guided Curriculum & Learning Centers

Hands-on literacy, math activities, art exploration, and small group teacher time.

11:00 AM

Outdoor Playground Discovery

Fresh air, motor skills exercise, sandbox, and active group games.

12:00 PM

Lunch Time & Rest Prep

Balanced, healthy lunch followed by story time and wind-down for nap time.

12:30 PM

Quiet Rest & Nap Time

Children recharge with soothing lullabies and restful sleep.

3:00 PM

Afternoon Snack & STEM Activities

Healthy afternoon snack followed by sensory play, storytime, and afternoon outdoor fun until pickup (6:30 PM).
